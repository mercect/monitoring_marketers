When you arrive at the office

Before anyone makes a call, in this order. Be ready to hand out materials and check attendance as the enumerators come in.

  1. Organize the tracking sheets The tracking sheets you received belong to two lists: 12Nov-D4 and 12Nov-D5. Each sheet says which list it is on.
  2. Check attendance: Start of the Day form Each enumerator sends the Start of the Day form next to you, one by one. Nobody starts calling without doing this. The check-ins then show in 🗓️ Attendance.
  3. Assign one tracking sheet and one case, in order Give each enumerator the next tracking sheet in order, and record the same pid in 🆕 To be assigned in the dashboard. The order is set in the dashboard (ordering) and is also printed on the tracking sheet, so the two should always match. One case at a time.

Monday 16 Nov: only the D4 list

On Monday we work ONLY the 12Nov-D4 list. Keep every 12Nov-D5 sheet to one side.
D4 sheets
Put them in order, using the order number on each tracking sheet. This is the pile you assign from.
D5 sheets
Set them aside, also in order, ready for the day they are due. Nobody calls them on Monday.
Dashboard

What's changed at a glance

The short version, for anyone who only reads one part.

The Data Team validates closures

Every 0_ closure an enumerator logs, every partial save to resume and every hard-to-reach case is checked by the Data Team in 🛂 Supervisor review. Each one is confirmed and closed, or sent back with a note.

Sent-back cases come first

A case the Data Team sends back goes to the top of ✅ Active pids, under 🔁 To be revised, with a note saying what to do. Call these before anything else.

First calls are assigned in the dashboard

New pids are handed out from 🆕 To be assigned, strictly in list order. Every assignment is logged, and calls are checked against it.

New Attendance tab

Who was planned, who checked in, and which list each person actually called.

A new first page

🧾 Calls summary now reads Do now → To fix → Progress, one line per call list.

Filter by call list

The filter at the top picks call lists by assigned_list (e.g. 12Nov-D4). The route filter, the Tracking sheet and the By enumerator tab are gone.

Who does what

Supervisors

  • Check 🗓️ Attendance at the start of each shift.
  • Hand out first calls in 🆕 To be assigned, in order.
  • After the first call, put each tracking sheet at the back of the pile, in the order it comes back.
  • Make sure 🔁 To be revised cases are called first and the note is read.
  • Follow up everything under ⚠️ To fix.
  • Leave closures with the Data Team codes (0_NA 0_OF 0_D 0_SC) to the Data Team.

Enumerators

  • Keep three piles of tracking sheets for each list they manage: reschedules, active, inactive.
  • Work ✅ Active pids from the top down.
  • Read supervisor_note before calling a pid that has one.
  • Close with their own codes only: 0_R 0_UN 0_IN 0_WN 0_OC.
  • Call only pids they were assigned, or that sit in a queue.

Data Team

  • Validates cases in 🛂 Supervisor review.
  • Confirms or corrects closures, closes hard cases, sends cases back with a note.
  • Reopens a closed case when needed.
  • Is the only one who can save in that tab.

The day, in order

The order to open things in. It follows the dashboard from top to bottom.

  1. 🗓️ AttendanceEveryone has sent the Start of the Day form next to you. Is everyone who was planned checked in, on the right list?
  2. 🧾 Calls summary → ⚠️ To fixAnything sent back by the Data Team, or calls that should not have happened.
  3. ✅ Active pids → 🔁 To be revisedCases the Data Team sent back. Called first, note read.
  4. ⏰ To be followed upCallbacks, overdue (red) first.
  5. ▶️ To be resumedPartial saves, from the tablet that holds them.
  6. 🆕 To be assignedFirst calls, in list order. Red rows (skipped) first.
  7. 📵 To keep callingEverything else still open, fewest attempts first.

What is final Changed

Completed
Final. Calls logged after it are ignored and flagged under ⚠️ To fix.
Enumerator 0_
Not final until the Data Team validates it. Until then, a new call reopens the case.
Data Team 0_
Final. A later call does not reopen it, and it is flagged under ⚠️ To fix.

🔁 Reopen a final closure · 📜 Log

Reopen
The Data Team can put a final closure back to work with a reason. It appears in 🔁 To be revised. Completed interviews cannot be reopened.
Log
Every decision, newest first. Nothing is overwritten: a change of mind is a new entry.
