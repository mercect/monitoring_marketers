Basics

  • Filters. The dashboard opens on the newest recruitment batch (date and route, in the sidebar). Widen either filter to see earlier batches.
  • Refresh now re-reads the Google Sheet. Otherwise the data refreshes by itself every 5 minutes.
  • Who is counted. Only people recruited as Eligible – signed up. Refusals and ineligibles at recruitment cannot be called, so they are left out of every figure.
  • "Now". Overdue and "due in the next hour" are measured against the latest submission in the data, not the clock. During field hours the two are minutes apart.

Call-list order

Each recruitment day's sign-ups are split into two call lists by assigned_day (4 or 5), and ordering is each pid's position in its list, from the randomisation. First attempts follow this order strictly, and so does the next-attempt pile: the day-4 list before the day-5 list, each in ordering. Both columns come from the data entry sheet.

Shift windows

shifts_to_try lists the windows nobody has called in yet, or says All tried / None tried. Spread calls across windows: someone who never answers in the morning may answer in the evening.

What is final

  • A completion is final. Calls logged after it are ignored, and flagged on the Calls summary.
  • A supervisor's closure is final. A call logged after it does not reopen the case. A completion still beats an earlier closure.
  • An enumerator's closure is not final until the supervisor validates it. Until then, a new call reopens the case.

✅ Active pids — the work queues

Every open pid sits in one queue. Work through them from the top. Each table starts with assigned_day (the day-4 or day-5 call list), then the pid. If the supervisor has left a note on a pid, it appears as supervisor_note right after the pid: read it before calling.

📵 To keep calling

Codes 2_IN 2_NA 2_OF 2_WN

No answer or phone off, or a wrong number or wrong person with other numbers still to try. The list starts with the fewest attempts; within the same number of attempts, it follows the call-list order (assigned_day, then ordering). Take the top row.

121 attempt only

After ordering come the shift windows not tried yet (shifts_to_try) and whether the respondent ever picked up (ever_picked_up). Then the totals: all calls (total_attempts) and how many ended in no answer (times_noanswer), phone off (times_off), a reschedule (times_rescheduled) or a drop (times_dropped). Someone who has picked up before but keeps rescheduling needs a different approach from someone whose phone is always off.

The last three columns describe the last call: last_callcode, last_enumerator and last_comment.

numbers_wrong is the most numbers any single call found wrong. The form counts per call, not in total, so this never counts the same wrong number twice.

🆕 To be assigned

Eligible sign-ups nobody has called yet. Hand them out strictly by ordering: the top row is always the next pid to give out. Never skip ahead (a 5 before a 3), and never repeat a case while new ones are waiting. days_waiting counts the days since recruitment.

A pid more than 8 places behind it (one round of 8 enumerators) was passed over. Example: the team is on #20–28 and #8 has never been called. The skipped column says how far the list has got. Hand these out first.
  • Position (one tile per call list): the highest number tried so far in each list, with next up (the lowest number not yet called, who to hand out next) and how many are left. Watch these closely in the first days: the front should move forward steadily, one round at a time.

Assigning in the dashboard

  1. Tick the pids you are handing out, lowest ordering first.
  2. Choose the enumerator for each in assign to.
  3. Type your name once (it goes into the log), then click Assign.

Each assignment is recorded in the decisions tab of the Google Sheet: when (Sierra Leone time), who assigned it, the pid and the enumerator. Assigned pids leave this list. Only first calls are assigned here.

Out of order: if you tick a pid while a lower number in the same list is still waiting, the dashboard says which, and asks for a reason before saving. The reason goes into the log.

💾 Partial saves

A partial save is stored on the tablet it was taken on and can only be resumed from that tablet. This tab shows which tablet holds each one and who to ask.

4held now
3tablets holding them
1no tablet id
  • What counts: the pid's last submission is a 4_ (4_D held partial, 4_SC upcoming reschedule, 4_NA / 4_OF a further attempt on a held case). Only what is still outstanding is listed.
  • No tablet id: it cannot be traced to a tablet. Start from the enumerator who last submitted.
  • A tablet id that is not a tablet: a callcode or the pid was typed in the tablet box. Raise it with the enumerator.
  • By tablet: enumerators are everyone who last submitted on a pid held by that tablet.

👤 By enumerator

  • On the plate now: active pids each enumerator holds (they made the last submission). resume (4_) are partial saves on their tablet; follow-up are 3_SC / 3_D. The two add up to the total.
  • Callback times: earliest first. ⚠ means overdue; a time with no date means today.
  • Historic effort: every call is credited to whoever made it. pids tried overlaps between enumerators, because several people try the same respondent.

📋 Tracking sheet

One row per respondent. Current state columns come from the most recent submission; history columns add up all submissions. Respondents nobody has called yet show an empty current state.

🛂 Supervisor review

For the supervisor. Everyone can see this tab; only the supervisor can save, with a separate editor password. It covers the whole roster: the sidebar filters do not apply here.

Validate closures

Every case an enumerator closed (0_) or completed (1) that has not been reviewed.

ActionResult
ConfirmThe closure or completion becomes final. A later call does not reopen it; it is flagged instead.
CorrectChanges the code, and is final. A wrong complete is corrected to a 0_ code.
Send backThe case returns to the queue it came from, and the comment appears to the enumerators as a note.

To clear a backlog, tick several rows and use Confirm selected.

CloseEnds the case with a 0_ code. Final.

Reviewing one pid

  1. Pick the pid. Its history appears first: attempts, last real call, reschedules, drops, shifts not yet tried, partial saved.
  2. Open All observations to see every submission. Grey rows are Communications form submissions (timing or status only, never interview answers); blue rows are earlier dashboard entries.
  3. Choose the action, the code if needed, write the comment, and save.

Every save adds one row to the decisions tab of the Google Sheet. Nothing is overwritten: changing your mind means a new entry, and the newest one for a pid counts. Do not edit that tab by hand. The Log shows every entry, newest first.

Counts shown in the flag examples are illustrations, not current figures.
